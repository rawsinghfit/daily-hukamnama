Privacy Policy

Daily Hukamnama · Last updated 24 October 2026

Daily Hukamnama shows the daily Hukamnama from Sri Darbar Sahib and can send you a notification when a new one is available. This page explains what information is used when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your reading settings are stored only on your device.

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, the app subscribes your device to a single notification topic through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging so that it can receive the daily Hukamnama notification. To deliver notifications, Firebase uses a device token and basic device information, as described in Privacy and Security in Firebase. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app's settings or in your phone's settings.

Hukamnama text (BaniDB)

The Hukamnama is loaded from the BaniDB API run by Khalis Foundation. Your internet address is visible to their servers as part of any normal web request.

Fonts

Fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts. Google receives standard request information such as your internet address.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, internet address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings, under "Ad privacy settings". On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

The web version of the app does not show ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.
